How this meter measures, and which targets it uses

LUFS (loudness units) is how YouTube, Facebook and broadcasters measure loudness (ITU-R BS.1770 / EBU R128). Short-term is the last 3 seconds, momentary is the last 0.4 seconds, and integrated is the average since you last pressed Reset. Press Reset at the start of each service.

True peak (dBTP) catches peaks that fall between samples, which turn into crackle after the platform re-encodes your audio. Keep stream true peaks below −1 dBTP.

LRA (loudness range) shows how much the level swings, for example between speaking and full-band songs. Above about 15 LU, viewers end up reaching for their volume control.

The house mix meter reads the electrical level of your mix. To measure how loud the room actually is, use the Room meter with a microphone and calibrate it against an SPL meter or phone SPL app.

Before using any reading, set the input in your computer's sound settings to a fixed level. Turn off all “enhancements”, automatic gain and noise suppression. This page asks the browser to turn them off, but some drivers keep them on.
